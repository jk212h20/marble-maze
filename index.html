How to play

  • Grip the handle — or drag anywhere, or use Arrow keys / WASD. Hold Shift for fine control.
  • Drag on the board — tilt with the pointer (phones and tablets too).
  • Tilt button — use the device's orientation sensor, if it has one.
  • R restart · Esc pause · D watch the solver · M mute · F frame meter
  • The marble is a steel ball on a real board: it keeps rolling, so slow down before corners.
  • Holes end the attempt; the brass-ringed cup is the goal. The board edge has a rim here — later levels may not.
